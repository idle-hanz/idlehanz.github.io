6. **No YouTube moment.** Good. Keep it out.

### Keep from Feb (author-shaped; do not let May overwrite)

1. User-typed Ch2 **summary** if a lead-in is used.

### Do not take from May without the author saying so

- “This is more biology than malice.” — ChatGPT cut; May put it back.

- “The herd needs both. Without erosion, it stagnates. Without restoration, it destroys itself.” — ChatGPT warned mechanistic phrases can be read as “cruelty is necessary.” PARK.

### Suggested intro softening (Grok-drafted, not locked)

Suggested: “We like to think we’re evolution’s crowning achievement — the smartest, most moral thing the universe has managed to produce. That comforting story is harder to maintain once you look closely.”

Park until an intro rewrite. Target *unconscious* exceptionalism, not biologists.
