# PIN — Meditation sits (parked)

Status: **parked**. Do not write scripts, do not record, do not build a course.
Pinned: 2 Oct 2026, from the original brainstorm thread after the author started Sam Harris’s Waking Up.

## Why it exists

Author is resonating with the core of Waking Up: consciousness is what is being experienced; the idea that we are our thoughts is an illusion. They want to resurrect *Meditation Through Nihilism* as an actual meditation series, produced with Suno (which now does voice). Something a person can do **once they have read the book**.

Author’s condition for coming back to this: sit with the book again and flesh it out past the current chapters. The later writing is what gives the sits their accent. Without that, the meditations would be Harris with a darker coat, not this book.

## What was agreed (do not reopen unless the author does)

- Not an audiobook of the chapters. Not a course that re-teaches the argument.
- Eight sits, 10–15 minutes, dry voice, long pauses. Call them **sits**, not lessons. “Course” pulls guru cadence.
- Assumes the reader already has Roar and Quiet loaded.
- Harris is the bridge (sit 1), not the destination. After the self thins out, Roar and Quiet are still both in the room. Neither is you. Neither is a villain. Seeing that does not float you above the harm. Ordinary hands remain.
- The original move Harris does not make: the grab for a controller (serpent, “they,” enlightenment-as-self) is the same grab. Sit 7 is the one that makes the series this book.

### The eight sits

1. What is appearing. Sounds, body, thoughts as contents. Do not name Roar or Quiet yet.
2. The thought that says “I.” The narrator is one more appearance.
3. The Roar in the body. Belonging, disgust, the urge to agree. Heavy and suffocating, not exciting. May tone.
4. The Quiet as the other pressure. Simultaneous, small, in the gut. Not holy, not a delay, not a command.
5. Both at once. You are not the winner of that argument.
6. The mirror. One memory of having eroded someone. No monster story, no self-hatred.
7. No controller. Let the mind reach for who is doing this. Look. Weather, not a villain. The embarrassment is the point.
8. A footnote. One small restoration you could actually do today. Not a vow. Then get up. The herd is still there.

### Refusals

- Quiet as enlightenment, or as “your true self.”
- Bells, ocean beds, a voice that lifts on the moral line.
- Andrew Tate energy or sermon energy.
- Public sits on Jews, religion, or parliament-TV material.

### Suno

Beds and the existing songs: yes. Speaking voice: only if it stays flat, slow, and slightly tired. Meditation lives in the pauses; generated speech fills them and performs wisdom. If it sounds like a wellness app, use the author’s own voice, even rough.

## When this is unpinned

Only after the book has been sat with and written further, so the sits inherit that accent.

First work then, and only this: draft **sit 1** and **sit 7** in the book’s register, pauses marked. Record those two. Wait a week. If they still sound like the author, do the rest. Do not write all eight in one pass.
